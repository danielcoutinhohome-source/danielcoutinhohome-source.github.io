Redline

What it is

An AI-powered iPad note-taking app that checks handwritten math step by step and shows you exactly where you went wrong.

How it works

Students work problems by hand on an iPad. Gemini reads each line and flags the step with the error, ElevenLabs talks the student through the fix out loud, and Redline turns every mistake into personalized practice problems and learning insights.

By the numbers
  • 24 hrsfrom idea to shipped app
  • 3person team
  • 2AI engines: Gemini for errors, ElevenLabs for voice
The idea

Most tools only grade the final answer. Redline finds the exact step where the reasoning broke, because that is where a student actually learns.

Pair Trading Algorithm

What it is

A Python analysis tool that finds two stocks that move together and flags when their prices drift far enough apart to trade.

How it works

It pulls five years of historical market data for any two tickers, checks their correlation, and fits a regression of one on the other. The spread becomes a z-score, and mean-reversion signals mark entry and exit points across strong buy, buy, hold, sell, and strong sell zones.

By the numbers
  • 5 yrof daily closes per ticker
  • 0.80minimum |correlation| to trade a pair
  • 5signal zones on the z-score
What I learned

A midpoint line looked right but ignored price scale and fired false signals, so I replaced it with a real regression. Using the absolute value of correlation let inversely correlated pairs work too.

Bitcoin Trading Bot

What it is

An automated Bitcoin trading bot, written in Pine Script, that logs every trade to a live Google Sheets dashboard.

How it works

The strategy uses technical indicators to trade volatility breakouts, with ATR-based stops and targets. Trade signals fire a webhook into Google Sheets, which logs each execution, calculates profit and loss metrics like Sharpe ratio and expectancy, and monitors performance in real time.

By the numbers
  • 24/7trading, since crypto never closes
  • 2 : 1reward-to-risk on every trade
  • $5,041largest trade, caught in a flash crash
What I learned

The first strategy lost money for days. Iterative backtesting and live-market refinement turned it into a breakout model that profits from big moves, and a unique key per trade stopped duplicate alerts from corrupting the data.

Reinforcement Learning Racing Agent

What it is

An AI that teaches itself to drive a racing track from scratch, using nothing but rewards and trial and error.

How it works

A Deep Q-Learning agent built in TensorFlow and Keras drives a Pygame racing track. NumPy arrays hold its state, epsilon-greedy exploration starts it random and makes it smarter each generation, and checkpoint-based rewards push it forward.

By the numbers
  • 1,000+generations trained
  • ~950gen where it found the optimal line
  • 20 hrsof training cut into a timelapse
What I learned

At one point the agent learned that crashing immediately cost the least reward. Rebuilding the reward function around checkpoints taught me that in AI, the bug is often in how you framed the problem.

AI-Powered Computer Vision Turret

What it is

A real-time turret with three modes: manual control, hand tracking that fires when you make a fist, and face tracking that recognizes specific people.

How it works

Built in Python with OpenCV and MediaPipe hand tracking. A closed fist triggers firing and pyUSB drives the hardware. A TensorFlow face recognition model, trained on a custom image dataset, identifies and tracks individuals in live video.

By the numbers
  • 21hand landmarks tracked per frame
  • 3modes: manual, hand, face
  • 20days, the longest build
What I learned

Smooth aim took constant testing and parameter tuning. The models are only half the job; the other half is making the hardware keep up with what the camera sees.

Stock Screening System

What it is

A Python platform that screens hundreds of public companies and ranks them, built to pick stocks for the Wharton investment competition.

How it works

It pulls fundamentals through financial APIs and runs every company through 20+ filters across valuation, profitability, growth, and leverage, then scores and ranks them to automate equity research.

By the numbers
  • 20+fundamental filters
  • 100sof companies analyzed
  • 4categories: valuation, profitability, growth, leverage
What I learned

At first the filters rejected every stock. Testing against companies I knew exposed the bugs, like debt-to-equity never being divided by 100 and missing data counting as a failure.
